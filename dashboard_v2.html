Regulatory Compliance
🇪🇸 Spain Legal Framework
COMPLIANT
Real Decreto 244/2019
Regulates collective self-consumption installations. VOLTCORE implements the required democratic governance, transparent settlement, and CNMC reporting obligations automatically.
Real Decreto 23/2020
Energy storage regulation. Off-grid communities with battery storage comply with safety, metering and reporting requirements through our IoT integration layer.
CNMC Reporting
Spain's energy regulator requires quarterly production and settlement reports. VOLTCORE auto-generates CNMC-format reports from on-chain settlement data.
🇪🇺 EU Legal Framework
COMPLIANT
RED II (2018/2001)
Renewable Energy Communities Directive. VOLTCORE provides the governance infrastructure that RED II mandates — member voting, transparent accounts, democratic control.
EU ETS Carbon Market
Verified renewable generation creates carbon credits under EU ETS. VOLTCORE tracks production, calculates CO₂ avoided, and prepares credit issuance documentation.
Guarantees of Origin (GO)
EU Directive 2009/28/EC. Every MWh of community solar generates a tradeable GO certificate. VOLTCORE mints these as on-chain verifiable credentials.
