Regulatory Compliance
🇪🇸 Spain Legal Framework
COMPLIANT
Real Decreto 244/2019
Regulates collective self-consumption. VOLTCORE implements democratic governance, transparent settlement, and CNMC reporting automatically via smart contracts.
Real Decreto 23/2020
Energy storage regulation. Off-grid communities with batteries comply via IoT integration with safety and metering requirements.
CNMC Reporting
Quarterly production and settlement reports auto-generated from on-chain settlement data. Tamper-proof and auditable.
🇪🇺 EU Legal Framework
COMPLIANT
RED II (2018/2001)
Renewable Energy Communities Directive. VOLTCORE provides the governance infrastructure RED II mandates — member voting, transparent accounts, democratic control.
EU ETS Carbon Market
Verified renewable generation tracked on-chain. CO₂ avoided calculated per settlement. Carbon credit documentation prepared automatically.
Guarantees of Origin
Every MWh of community solar generates a tradeable GO certificate. Minted as on-chain verifiable credentials via Solana.
